Advancing the Integrated Application Scenarios of “AI + Power Systems” in Qingdao

August 4, 2026 · 1 min read · Aochen SUN

Integrating AI into the energy industry is essential to cultivating new quality productive forces and building a new-type energy system. The power system, which spans the entire chain of generation, transmission, transformation, distribution, and consumption, offers the most concentrated AI use cases and the most mature enabling technologies of any energy segment. Deploying more integrated applications in the “AI + Power Systems” serves several strategic goals at once. It advances the national “AI+” initiative and the strategic plan for building a strong energy nation, and it allows Qingdao to capitalize on its designation as a pilot city for new-type power systems. It will also strongly support the city’s green development during the 15th Five-Year Plan period and help foster emerging “AI+” industries…
